# Scoring rubric

Single source of truth for the Skill, the GPT, and `score.py`. Five dimensions, each scored 0 (reactive/absent) to 3 (fully proactive), summed to a 0-15 Commercial Proactivity Score.

This rubric encodes one idea, repeated five ways: **did the CSM/AM drive the conversation forward, or only respond to what the customer brought up.** That is the "F: From reactive to commercial" mindset shift at the center of the SHIFT Method, applied to a single call instead of a whole team.

## 1. Renewal ownership

*Did the CSM/AM proactively raise renewal timeline, process, or next steps, rather than waiting for the customer to ask?*

- **0** - Renewal not mentioned at all, or the customer had to raise it first
- **1** - Mentioned briefly, no timeline or process attached
- **2** - CSM raised it proactively, with a rough timeline
- **3** - CSM raised it proactively, with a specific timeline, process, and named stakeholders

## 2. Risk surfacing

*Did the CSM name a risk, blocker, or area of concern before the customer did?*

- **0** - No risks discussed, or the customer raised a concern the CSM had not anticipated
- **1** - CSM acknowledged a risk only after the customer raised it
- **2** - CSM named a risk proactively, but without a mitigation plan
- **3** - CSM named a risk proactively, with a specific mitigation plan and an owner

## 3. Expansion probing

*Did the CSM actively probe for expansion signals: new use cases, other teams/departments, growth plans, usage constraints?*

- **0** - No expansion-oriented questions asked
- **1** - One generic question ("anything else we can help with?")
- **2** - Specific expansion question asked, no follow-through
- **3** - Specific expansion question asked, with a concrete next step (demo, intro, proposal)

## 4. Stakeholder mapping

*Did the CSM ask about or reference other stakeholders, an executive sponsor, or the decision process?*

- **0** - No stakeholders beyond the person on the call discussed
- **1** - Stakeholders mentioned in passing, by either party
- **2** - CSM proactively asked about additional stakeholders or sponsor engagement
- **3** - CSM has a clear, named map of who else is involved and their role

## 5. Next-step ownership

*Did the call end with a specific, owned, dated next step, or a vague "let me know if you need anything"?*

- **0** - No next step, or purely customer-owned ("we'll get back to you")
- **1** - Vague next step, no owner or date
- **2** - Specific next step, owner named, no date
- **3** - Specific next step, both owner and date named

## Bands

| Score | Band | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| 13-15 | Commercial | Fully proactive, SHIFT-aligned. This is what "operating commercially, not just relationally" sounds like on a real call. |
| 8-12 | Developing | Real proactive moments, inconsistent. Some dimensions are strong, others still reactive. |
| 4-7 | Reactive | Mostly responding to the customer, rarely driving. The relationship is fine; the commercial mandate is not being exercised. |
| 0-3 | Order-taking | Pure support/relationship mode. No commercial ownership visible in this call. |

Two things get extracted separately and are not part of the score: **expansion opportunities** (quoted, with a one-line reason each) and **risk/churn signals** (quoted, with a one-line reason each). A call can score low on the rubric and still surface a real expansion signal, or score high and still miss one -- the score measures the CSM's behavior, the extraction measures what was actually said.
